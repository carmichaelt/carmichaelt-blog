Scratch to reveal, track progress, and swap finished books, films, games, and the rest. The original idea depended on editorial taste and curated lists; later work leaned into second-hand swaps and social features.

Currently scaling back. It did not find enough paying users against free competitors, and agents could not replace the human curator the first version needed.
