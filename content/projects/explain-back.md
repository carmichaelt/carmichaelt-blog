A GCSE revision app built around teaching, not quizzes. In Teach Mode the student explains a topic out loud to a curious listener. In Learn Mode the AI coaches the parts that did not hold up. Each session ends with coaching-style feedback and one next practice step.

Currently scaling back. The product still runs, with learner, guardian, and organisation paths on WorkOS and Polar.
