Plan a trip with friends. An organiser creates a trip and shares a link. Friends join with a display name — no account required — then add ideas, vote, comment, and build a dated itinerary together.

Live updates, guest sessions, and an AI scheduling assistant sit on Convex. The organiser keeps the invite link; everyone else just needs the URL.
